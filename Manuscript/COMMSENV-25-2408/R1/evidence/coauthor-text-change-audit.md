## Scope and decision rule

This is an editorial audit of the submitted article in Original/ against the current R1 article source, including title, abstract, main text, Methods, statements, main figures, and supplementary captions and tables. It also checks whether the response letter's stated reasons match the article. It does not approve the revised science or change the manuscript. Line references below refer to the current source files, not rendered PDF lines. The revised reference bibliography was audited separately in terminology-audit.md.

**Keep** means the wording or an equivalent factual statement is needed to describe an altered analysis, figure, result, explicitly requested reviewer clarification, or a material limit on inference. **Narrow** means the factual point is needed but should be inserted into the co-author-approved sentence with fewer other changes. **Restore** means the new wording is stylistic, introduces a new interpretation without a corresponding test, or drops approved context without a review reason. **Verify** means a factual discrepancy must be checked against the analysis before either wording is chosen. These judgments are editorial, not a substitute for co-author approval.

The principal finding is that the new analytical and validation material warrants a substantial Results and Methods revision, but the Introduction, much of the Discussion, and the entire Abstract were rewritten more broadly than necessary. A minimal-change revision can retain the approved argumentative order while correcting overclaims and adding the new evidence.

## Follow-up: PAP display and model-fit summaries

- **Remove special emphasis on $N_1-N_2$.** This difference remains one of the ten analysed PAPs, with its definition in Methods and its individual supplementary trajectory. It has no distinct role in H1 or H2 interpretation, so the Introduction and Results no longer single it out. The six-panel main figure now shows the familiar Shannon-effective diversity ($N_1$) in its place; the underlying PAP analyses and ten supplementary trajectories are unchanged.
- **Report model fit with genuine intervals.** The original available spatial models (1,156 sequences) have mean total adjusted $R^2$ 0.352 [0.326, 0.374]; the controlled spatial subset (1,098 sequences) has mean 0.440 [0.426, 0.449]. Brackets are 95% percentile confidence intervals from 10,000 bootstrap draws resampling the observed regions within each continental group as blocks. The separate 0.450 [0.326, 0.567] summary is a median and interquartile range across controlled sequences, not a confidence interval. The existing model fits and conclusions were not recalculated.
- **Limits of the intervals.** Region-block resampling retains sequences together within each pragmatic region and keeps all five continental groups represented, but it does not include model uncertainty or dependence between regions. Methods now states this explicitly, and the active reviewer response uses the same mean/CI and median/IQR labels.
