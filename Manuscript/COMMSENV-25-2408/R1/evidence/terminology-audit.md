# Terminology audit for the revised article and response

This internal table compares the submitted article in `../../Original/` with the R1 article and point-by-point response. It governs author-written prose, captions, and displayed table labels. Reviewer comments and reference titles remain verbatim. Code identifiers and source filenames remain stable where they link to analytical outputs.

| Concept | Submitted wording | R1 wording before this audit | Preferred wording | Meaning and exceptions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Individual fossil pollen time series | "pollen sequence" | "sequence" and "record" | **pollen sequence** at first mention; **sequence** thereafter | One dated pollen series, not an individual sample. Retain "record" only for a different archive, an established citation title, or verbatim reviewer text. |
| Individual observation within a sequence | "sample" or "assemblage" | "sample" | **sample** | A sampled level with a pollen count and age estimate. "Assemblage" refers to the pollen composition of that sample, not the whole sequence. |
| Initial analytical collection | "1,262 sequences available for analyses" | "original H1 model inventory" | **1,262 sequences considered for the initial spatial analysis** | An initial set, not a claim that every sequence produced an estimable decomposition or entered every later comparison. The 1,270-sequence preparation pool remains separate. |
| Analysis-specific selection | Usually implicit | "cohort", "eligible cohort", "matched cohort" | **analysis subset** or **matched sequences/comparisons** | The unit is stated with its count: sequences for spatial analyses and continental-group time intervals for temporal analyses. "Cohort" remains only in immutable data identifiers if present. |
| Broad geographical grouping | "continents" and "continental regions" | "continental groups" | **continental groups** | North America, Central and South America, Europe, Asia, and Oceania. These are broad analysis groups, not claims about continent boundaries. |
| Smaller analytical grouping | "climate zones" and "sub-regions" | "regions" | **regions** | Practical subdivisions nested within continental groups, assigned using present-day Köppen–Geiger classes. They are neither reconstructed Holocene climate zones nor necessarily continuous natural units. Retain "climate zones" only when quoting or describing the submitted terminology. |
| Pollen response domain | "vegetation assemblage dynamics" and "pollen assemblage properties" | "pollen assemblage dynamics", occasionally "taxon-specific vegetation responses" | **pollen assemblage properties (PAPs)** and **pollen assemblage dynamics** | Assemblage-level summaries may inform vegetation history. The fitted models do not estimate climate or human effects on individual pollen taxa; that would require a separate analysis. |
| Human population proxy | "validated proxies for regional human population changes" | "proxy for regional human population changes" | **SPDs as validated proxies for regional human population changes** | A proxy interpretation, not a population census; keep archaeological preservation and sampling limitations nearby. |
| Methods section | "Methods" | "Methods" in the article; "Supplementary Methods" in parts of the response | **Methods** | The article's full Methods section precedes the separately labelled Supplementary Information. Refer to its "Result-validation analyses" subsection by that title. |
