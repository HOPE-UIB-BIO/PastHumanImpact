# Reciprocal spatiotemporal control: manuscript-ready text

## Methods

We evaluated the H1 human-versus-climate comparison using reciprocal temporal
and spatial controls. We enforced one analytical row per pollen core and
500-year age, averaging repeated PAP response estimates only after confirming
that predictor values were invariant. Within cores, we retained the original
human-plus-climate model and fitted a three-group hierarchical partition with
human, climate, and centred and scaled linear age. Linear age was used instead
of temporal Moran eigenvectors because cores contained at most 14 unique ages.
Hierarchical contributions, pure partial adjusted-R-squared fractions, shared
variation, and residual temporal Moran diagnostics were reported separately.

For the spatial synthesis, positive distance-based Moran eigenvector maps
(dbMEMs) were constructed separately within continents and selected from the
signed time-controlled human-minus-climate difference conditional on
continent-by-climate-zone strata. Selected terms were reused for the bounded
presentation balance. Pooled and subgroup balances were evaluated at dbMEM
equal to zero and checked using 250-km and 500-km thinning plus continent and
climate-zone leave-out analyses.

For cross-site region-by-age models, positive dbMEMs were selected against the
multivariate PAP response conditional on complete human and climate predictor
groups. We retained the original two-group model, fitted a
human-climate-space hierarchical partition when spatial terms were selected,
and calculated pure human, climate, and spatial fractions using partial RDA.
No-signal groups retain their baseline human and climate shares with a zero
displayed spatial share; non-estimable groups remain missing.
