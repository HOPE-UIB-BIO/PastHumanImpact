# HVarPart figure and response revision

Date: 2026-07-29

This decision supersedes the figure-presentation and reviewer-response wording
in `plan_hvarpart_relative_importance_2026-07-29.md`.

## Reviewer-response scope

- The reviewers did not identify the loss of negative adjusted HVarPart
  contributions. The authors identified this error during additional
  quality-control checks.
- The response must describe the correction as author-identified and compare
  signed, zero-truncated, and exclude-negative profiles.
- Reporting the total variation explained by the models is deferred to a
  separate issue and is not claimed as an output of this work.

## Main and supplementary figures

- Main Figures 2--4 use zero-truncated allocations or transformations derived
  directly from them.
- Supplementary counterparts of Figures 2--4 show unmodified signed
  allocations over their full ranges using signed-safe geometries.
- The exclude-negative profile remains a sensitivity comparison rather than a
  separate main figure.

## Figure 2 design

- Express the complementary paired allocations as human minus climate
  importance.
- Retain the continent maps and canonical continent order.
- For each continent, show the distribution of record-level balances, pooled
  climate-zone balances as points and intervals, and the pooled continent
  balance as a horizontal line.
- Use a bounded scale from -1 (climate impact), through 0 (equal importance),
  to 1 (human impact), with a climate-neutral-human density gradient.
- Embed maps directly into the statistical facet rows so all continent panels
  align exactly.
- Retain the original three-part geometry with one shared complete signed
  range for the supplementary human-allocation figure.

## Figures 3 and 4 design

- Apply the same bounded human-minus-climate balance to temporal H1 summaries
  and H2 importance insets.
- Show temporal SPD and event balances as offset lollipops through time, with
  stems originating at equal importance and shapes distinguishing proxies.
- Replace the H2 main-figure inset bars with one balance lollipop per region
  and climate zone.
- Use the same climate-neutral-human point palette across Figures 2--4.
- Retain unmodified signed allocations in the supplementary counterparts.
